CAMP SCHEDULE & DAILY ITINERARY

Every hour is engineered for adventure, team building, and preparing campers for the big jump to high school.

⚡ TYPICAL DAILY RHYTHM

While each day features unique special events, our baseline daily routine balances active outdoor challenges with rest, social bonding, and safety safeguards.

MORNING

Breakfast, Cabin Inspection, Morning Rally, & High-Adventure Outdoor Rotations.

AFTERNOON

Lunch, Waterfront / Kayaking, Skill Workshops, & Daily Parent Call Window (04:45 PM – 05:30 PM).

EVENING

Themed Dinner Fest, Night Tournaments & Showcases, Campfire Reflection, & Cabin Lights Out.

5-DAY DETAILED PROGRAMME

DAY 1: ARRIVAL, BONDING & BBQ BASH

WELCOME & FOUNDATIONS
  • 01:00 PM – 02:30 PM
    Check-In & Cabin Orientation: Meet your counselors (1:8 ratio), move into cabins, and complete mandatory health check-in with our 24/7 RN.
  • 03:00 PM – 04:30 PM
    Icebreaker Games & Team Naming: Cabin challenges to set camp goals and forge early connections.
  • 04:45 PM – 05:30 PM
    📱 Daily Parent Call Window: Campers check in with home before daytime digital detox commences.
  • 06:00 PM – 07:30 PM
    Welcome BBQ Bash: Camp-wide kickoff dinner featuring custom options for all dietary needs.
  • 08:00 PM – 10:00 PM
    DIY Swag Screen-Printing & Opening Campfire: Custom-print camp t-shirts and join the opening night campfire ceremony.

DAY 2: HIGH ROPES & ESPORTS ARENA

COURAGE & STRATEGY
  • 08:30 AM – 12:00 PM
    ACCT Canopy Zipline & High Ropes: Certified guides lead teams through aerial obstacle courses to build confidence and resilience.
  • 01:30 PM – 04:30 PM
    Lake Adventures: Kayaking, paddleboarding, and water team races guarded by Red Cross certified lifeguards.
  • 04:45 PM – 05:30 PM
    📱 Daily Parent Call Window: 45-minute daily check-in window.
  • 06:00 PM – 07:30 PM
    Pasta Bar Dinner: Energy-packed feast with gluten-free & vegan stations.
  • 07:45 PM – 09:45 PM
    Switch Esports League: Organised team-based gaming tournament emphasizing communication and strategy.

DAY 3: MASTER CHEF & COMMUNITY IMPACT

CREATIVITY & LEADERSHIP
  • 09:00 AM – 12:00 PM
    Community Impact Projects: Collaborative service project giving back to local forest conservation and trail restoration.
  • 01:30 PM – 04:30 PM
    High School Readiness Workshops: Interactive group discussions on navigating high school social circles, time management, and self-advocacy.
  • 04:45 PM – 05:30 PM
    📱 Daily Parent Call Window: 45-minute daily check-in window.
  • 06:00 PM – 08:30 PM
    Master Chef Culinary Battle & Luau Night: Cabin teams prepare signature gourmet dishes over camp stoves, evaluated by counselor judges!
  • 09:00 PM – 10:00 PM
    Stargazing & Campfire Stories: Nighttime trail walk and campfire reflection.

DAY 4: FINAL CHALLENGE & NIGHT OF STARS

CELEBRATION GALA
  • 09:00 AM – 12:00 PM
    The Transformation Relay: Camp-wide signature team obstacle course combining land navigation, ropes skills, and team puzzle-solving.
  • 01:30 PM – 04:00 PM
    Free Choice Activity Hour & Gala Prep: Time to prep outfit/costumes for the evening banquet or enjoy open waterfront activities.
  • 04:45 PM – 05:30 PM
    📱 Daily Parent Call Window: Final daily check-in window before departure day.
  • 06:30 PM – 10:30 PM
    'Night of Stars' Gala Banquet & Dance: Celebration dinner, camper awards ceremony, talent showcase, and farewell dance party!

DAY 5: CLOSING CEREMONY & HIGH SCHOOL KICKOFF

READY FOR 9TH GRADE
  • 08:30 AM – 10:00 AM
    Farewell Breakfast & Cabin Packing: Cabin clean-up and gear packing.
  • 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM
    Closing Graduation Ceremony: Parents invited to join! Pinning ceremony, camp video recap reel, and camper high school pledges.
  • 12:00 PM
    Camp Departure: Pick-up and departure—ready to conquer 9th grade!
